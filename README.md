## 🧑‍💻 About Me

> *"Good architecture is not about chasing what's new — it's about knowing what's foundational."*

I'm **Manas Dash** — a **Software Architect with 21 years of hands-on production experience**, currently at **Tesco Technology, Bangalore**.

My foundation is **core computer science**: distributed systems, event-driven architecture, API design, and system thinking. These aren't stepping stones for me — they are the work. I've spent two decades building systems that are resilient, observable, and maintainable at retail scale, serving millions of customers across one of the world's largest retailers.

I believe **software and systems are the craft**. AI is a powerful capability I apply *through* that craft — not the other way around. I am not building models or fine-tuning weights. I am **implementing AI into real systems**: integrating LLMs into production pipelines, designing agentic workflows, and making conversational AI a reliable component in distributed architectures — the same way I've integrated Kafka, Redis, or any other tool that earns its place.

My philosophy: understand the core deeply, apply the new deliberately, and share everything openly.

**Currently focused on:** AI-implemented systems · Distributed architecture · Engineering leadership · Knowledge sharing
